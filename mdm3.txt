Step 1: Install the Wi-Fi Analyzer Application
Install a suitable Wi-Fi Analyzer application on the smartphone.

Step 2: Open the Application
Open the installed Wi-Fi Analyzer application.
Observe the main screen and the available Wi-Fi scanning or analysis options.

Step 3: Enable Wi-Fi
Enable Wi-Fi on the smartphone.
Make sure the smartphone is able to detect nearby wireless networks.

Step 4: Provide Required Permissions
Provide the permissions requested by the application, such as Location permission, if required by the smartphone or application.

Step 5: Start Wi-Fi Scanning
Start the Wi-Fi scan in the application to detect nearby wireless networks.

Step 6: Observe Detected Networks
Observe the list of Wi-Fi networks detected by the application.
For each detected network, observe the available information such as:
SSID 
RSSI 
Channel number 
Frequency band 

Step 7: Record the SSID
Select or note the SSID (name) of each detected Wi-Fi network.
Record the SSID in the observation table.

Step 8: Record RSSI
Observe the RSSI/signal strength of each detected network.
Record the RSSI value in dBm.

Step 9: Record Channel Number
Observe the Wi-Fi channel number used by each detected network.
Record the corresponding channel number in the observation table.

Step 10: Record Frequency Band
Observe the frequency band used by each Wi-Fi network, such as 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz.
Record the frequency band in the observation table.

Step 11: Open Channel Analysis
Open the channel graph or channel analysis view available in the Wi-Fi Analyzer application.
Observe how the detected networks are distributed across different Wi-Fi channels.

Step 12: Observe Channel Occupancy
Observe the number of nearby Wi-Fi networks operating on each channel.
Note which channels have a large number of networks and which have fewer networks.

Step 13: Identify Crowded and Less-Crowded Channels
Identify the most crowded and least crowded Wi-Fi channels based on the displayed channel analysis.

Step 14: Compare the Detected Networks
Compare the RSSI values, channels, frequency bands, and channel occupancy of the detected Wi-Fi networks.
Observe which networks have stronger or weaker signals and which channels are more crowded.

Step 15: Record Observations and Conclusion
Record all observations in the observation table.
Based on the obtained results, write the conclusion regarding the detected Wi-Fi networks, their signal strengths, frequency bands, and channel usage.
